# Flow of Solution

1.  **Create a research collection** --- Open a workspace for a selected
    research topic.
2.  **Upload multiple PDFs** --- Add up to 5 research papers.
3.  **Validate documents** --- Check file type, size, readability, and
    extractable text.
4.  **Extract paper information** --- Extract page text, abstracts,
    keywords, authors, publication years, objectives, methods, datasets,
    findings, and limitations where available.
5.  **Understand each paper** --- Generate a beginner-friendly
    explanation of each paper.
6.  **Discover research connections** --- Identify recurring themes,
    shared keywords, overlapping topics, and relationships between
    papers.
7.  **Compare methodologies and findings** --- Compare methods,
    datasets, results, and limitations side by side.
8.  **Explore the essential relationship graph** --- View paper and
    theme nodes connected by labelled relationships; search, filter,
    zoom, pan, and reset the view.
9.  **Inspect graph evidence** --- Select a node or relationship to open
    details and source-paper/page references.
10. **Explore conflicting results** --- Highlight differing findings and
    explain possible reasons using evidence from the papers.
11. **Identify gaps and limitations** --- Group common limitations and
    find topics that appear underexplored in the uploaded collection.
12. **Explore research trends** --- Display themes and keywords by
    publication year to show how they appear over time.
13. **Generate future directions** --- Suggest research directions and
    project ideas based on the identified gaps and limitations.
14. **Ask questions** --- Answer questions using the uploaded papers and
    provide supporting references.
15. **Verify insights** --- Open the cited paper/page to check the
    evidence.
