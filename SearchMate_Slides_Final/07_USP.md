# Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

## "Understand the connections between research papers, not just the papers themselves."

### Key unique features

-   **Multi-paper intelligence:** Analyse a collection of research
    papers together.
-   **Beginner-friendly explanations:** Make academic information easier
    to understand.
-   **Research relationship discovery:** Find recurring themes, shared
    keywords, overlapping topics, and methodology connections.
-   **Interactive relationship exploration:** Explore connections
    between papers and themes, with explanations and source links.
-   **Common limitation detection:** Identify limitations repeated
    across multiple papers.
-   **Underexplored topic discovery:** Highlight topics that appear
    often but receive limited exploration within the uploaded
    collection.
-   **Conflict explanation:** Compare differing findings and explain
    possible reasons supported by the papers.
-   **Research timeline:** Show how themes and keywords appear across
    publication years.
-   **Idea generation:** Use gaps and limitations to suggest possible
    future research directions and student project ideas.
-   **Evidence-grounded Q&A:** Ask questions and receive answers
    supported by uploaded papers and references.

### Interactive graph priority

**Essential MVP:** paper/theme nodes, labelled relationships,
click-to-view details, evidence references, zoom/pan/reset, search, and
core filters.

**Advanced post-MVP:** research-gap nodes, conflicting-finding links,
year filters, custom layouts, and relationship-strength visualization.

The graph must explain why items are connected and show evidence; it
must not treat visual proximity as proof.

### Core value

SearchMate connects paper understanding, comparison, gap discovery, and
research exploration in one guided workflow, while keeping important
insights traceable to their sources.

**Note:** These are planned product capabilities. They should not be
presented as implemented until verified in the application.
