# Difference Between SearchMate and Existing Solutions

### Common limitations the project aims to address

Research tools may focus mainly on summarising individual papers or
present dense academic graphs and technical information that are
difficult for beginners to interpret.

### Interactive graph as a usability difference

SearchMate's planned graph is intentionally evidence-oriented rather
than a decorative network: - Users can inspect paper and theme nodes and
labelled relationship lines. - Selecting a node or edge reveals its
details and supporting paper/page references. - Search, filters, zoom,
pan, and reset make a small collection easier to explore. - A list/table
alternative keeps the same essential information available without
requiring graph interaction.

The basic graph controls and paper/theme relationships are **Essential
MVP** scope. Research-gap nodes, conflicting-finding links, year
filters, custom layouts, and relationship-strength styling are
**Advanced post-MVP** scope.
