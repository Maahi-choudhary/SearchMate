# SearchMate

## AI-Assisted Research Paper Analysis

### Problem Statement

Students and beginner researchers often collect multiple research papers
but find it difficult to: - Understand complex academic language and
extract important information. - Identify recurring themes, keywords,
and overlapping topics. - Compare research methodologies, datasets, and
findings. - Understand why different papers report conflicting
results. - Recognise repeated limitations and topics that remain
underexplored. - Identify useful future research directions and project
ideas.

**SearchMate aims to turn a collection of research papers into an
understandable picture of the research area.**

**Interactive relationship exploration:** The planned graph view will
connect paper and theme nodes with evidence-backed relationship lines,
so users can inspect how ideas overlap instead of relying only on
isolated summaries.

**Target users:** College students, beginner researchers, and students
preparing mini-projects or literature reviews.
